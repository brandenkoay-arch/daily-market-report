**Date:** Current

### 1. BUSINESS QUALITY
* **Core Model:** Corpay operates a global spend management and payments platform, shifting from transactional fee models to recurring software-plus-service revenue.
* **Segment Structure:** Dominated by Corporate Payments (AP modernization, card spend, cross-border), Vehicle Payments, and Lodging/Logistics.
* **Competitive Advantage:** Highly fragmented industry with high switching costs. Once integrated into a company’s ERP and procurement workflow, churn is negligible. Corpay benefits from "land-and-expand" sales velocity in the AP Automation space.
* **Scale Effect:** As a leader in global corporate spend, Corpay leverages massive data sets to refine fraud detection and compliance algorithms, creating a data moat that smaller regional players cannot replicate.

### 2. EARNINGS QUALITY
* **EPS Trajectory:** YoY EPS growth of 36.5% is exceptional for a mature tech company. This reflects operational leverage as high-margin software revenue outpaces lower-margin transaction volume.
* **Margin Expansion:** Operating margins of ~47% and gross margins of ~80% indicate high quality of earnings. The shift from pure processing fees to SaaS/subscription models is driving sustainable margin accretion.
* **One-Time Items:** Recent news notes "profits take a hit" due to the divestiture of the UK fleet software business. While this creates a temporary noise in GAAP earnings, it is a strategic rationalization to focus on higher-growth core segments, not a sign of operational decay.
* **Cash Conversion:** Free Cash Flow of ~$1.95B on a $27B market cap represents a ~7.1% FCF Yield. This provides balance sheet strength to fuel buybacks and M&A.

### 3. REVENUE ACCELERATION
* **Growth Rate:** Revenue growth of 21.5% is robust for a company of this size ($7–8B revenue base implied by P/S 5.4x and Market Cap $27B).
* **Drivers:**
* **Corporate Payments:** Strong demand for AP modernization and cross-border payment solutions as companies adapt to global supply chains and rising inflationary scrutiny on vendor spend.
* **Vehicle/Lodging:** Inflationary tailwinds in fuel/toll prices and corporate travel recovery provide natural top-line inflation.
* **Forward Outlook:** CFO Peter Walker explicitly stated expectations for "growth momentum to continue through the second half of the year," citing strong customer activity and retention. The 2026 outlook has been characterized as "stronger," suggesting the quarter-over-quarter acceleration is real, not a one-off.

### 4. COMPETITIVE MOAT
* **Switching Costs:** High. Integration with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, and other ERPs makes switching painful and costly.
* **Network Effects/Scale:** Global payment rails and compliance data require significant capital to build. Corpay’s existing infrastructure lowers marginal costs for new customers.
* **Brand & Trust:** In payments, trust is the product. Corpay’s established presence with Fortune 500 logos creates a barrier to entry for fintech startups targeting enterprise spend management.
* **Data Moat:** Proprietary data on global spending patterns improves risk scoring and procurement insights, adding value beyond mere payment processing.

### 5. INDUSTRY TAILWINDS
* **AP Modernization Wave:** Enterprises are aggressively digitizing Accounts Payable to catch-check fraud, improve DPO (Days Payable Outstanding), and gain visibility into non-card spend. Corpay is a primary beneficiary.
* **Cross-Border Complexity:** Geopolitical fragmentation is making cross-border payments more complex and expensive, driving demand for specialized platforms that handle currency conversion, compliance, and speed.
* **Corporate Travel Recovery:** As business travel normalizes, the Logistics segment (Lodging) is seeing renewed volume, aided by pricing power in a post-pandemic labor market for travel services.
* **News Context:** Recent reports highlight that "Corporate Payments takes center stage" in sustaining momentum. The industry trend is shifting from simple card issuance to holistic spend intelligence, a niche Corpay dominates.

### 6. MANAGEMENT EXECUTION
* **Capital Allocation:** Aggressive buybacks (noted in recent news) are supporting EPS growth alongside organic expansion. With $1.9B FCF, the balance sheet can support continuous share reduction.
* **Strategic Focus:** The simplification of the business via the UK fleet sale demonstrates a willingness to prune low-growth or non-core assets to sharpen the investment thesis. This is a positive signal of discipline.
* **Guidance:** History of beating targets (Q2 beat) and raising outlooks. Management is confident in the "strong 2026 outlook."

### 7. VALUATION

* **PEG Ratio:** 0.8. A PEG < 1.0 typically signals undervaluation relative to growth.
* **Relative Valuation:** Comparables in the Spend Management space (e.g., Guidewire, TripActions, or fintech peers) often trade at 20–30x forward earnings. Corpay’s discount likely reflects its legacy transaction mix, but the transition to software is re-rating the multiple.
* **Technical Premium:** The stock is at $416.37, only 2.6% from its 52-week high. The low multiple is not due to stock de-rating; rather, the stock has been strong, but the growth acceleration has outpaced the multiple expansion. This suggests the "good" news is already priced in, but the valuation still offers a margin of safety if growth holds.

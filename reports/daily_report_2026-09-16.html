**REGIME:** MARKET IN DOWNTREND

### 1. WHAT CHANGED SINCE YESTERDAY
The market has formally broken down from "Uptrend Under Pressure" into a confirmed **Downtrend**, signaled by QQQ closing yesterday (9/15) **below its 50DMA** (-0.8% daily move) and SOXX crashing **-5.63% in one session**. This is no longer a correction; it is a structural break. The primary catalyst for this acceleration is the precursor to today's **FOMC rate decision** (CNBC, 0.4h ago), where the 10Y yield holding at **5.0%** (up 0.71%) indicates the bond market is pricing in a "higher for longer" stance rather than the cuts growth traders anticipated. The 2026-09-15 tape was noise; yesterday's tape revealed the fault line.

### 2. MARKET REGIME ASSESSMENT
The tape is telling us that the Fed is in no position to rescue risk assets, and the equity market is punishing high-multiple growth names for it. QQQ is down **-4.58% over 3 months** and off its highs by **-4.85%**, confirming a downtrend structure that invalidates most Minervini top-10 setups. With VIX at 17.2 (elevated for a risk-off scenario) and the 10Y yield at 5.0%, the discount rate is actively destroying net present value calculations for long-duration growth tech. The regime is **Defensive/De-risking**; the "Pivot" trade is dead for the moment.

### 3. SECTOR LEADERSHIP
* **Leading:** **Communication Services (XLC: +2.19% 1D)** and **Healthcare (XLV: +1.45% 1D)**. This is classic defensive rotation: money is fleeing Tech (XLK: -1.81%) and Industrials (XLI: -1.42%) to hide in XLC's lower beta and XLV's dividend stability. Note XLC has been positive for 1M (+1.88%) while Tech has bled (-3.02%).
* **Lagging:** **Semiconductors (SOXX: -5.63% 1D, -20.85% 3M)**. This is the epicenter of the crash. The 1M drop of -9.63% suggests a liquidity unwind, not just valuation reset. **Industrials (XLI: -1.42% 1D)** are also breaking down, likely due to the "House bill to rein in AI data center utility costs" (CNBC, 1.6h ago) hitting infrastructure capex sentiment.

### 4. NEWS-DRIVEN RISKS & CATALYSTS
1. **FOMC Rate Decision (Today, 2026-09-16):** With the 10Y yield at 5.0%, the market is primed for a **hawkish hold** or even a signal of hikes if inflation data (not released until 10/14) is implied to be sticky. A hawkish statement will likely push VIX above 25, triggering stop-outs in Growth stocks. *Watch for any deviation from "higher for longer" rhetoric; if they signal a cut, we get a dead-cat bounce. If they shudder at 5% yields, we get a leg down.*
2. **BOJ Step-Up (Upcoming, but priced in):** CNBC (1.5h ago) reports the BOJ is expected to hike to a **three-decade high**. This tightens global dollar liquidity and historically causes risk-off in US Tech. Combined with the FOMC, this is a **global rate shock** for growth equities.
3. **AI/Data Center Policy Headwind (CNBC, 1.6h ago):** The House vote on a bill to **rein in AI data center utility costs** is a direct threat to the margin expansion thesis for Magnificent 7 names. This isn't just a headline; it's a tax on capex. Coupled with Huang attending Trump's state dinner (3.8h ago), the government is getting involved in both the cost structure and the geopolitical positioning of Big Tech.

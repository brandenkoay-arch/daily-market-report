📊 Macro Analysis
🌍 MARKET IN DOWNTREND ▼
**1. WHAT CHANGED SINCE YESTERDAY**
The regime has collapsed from "Uptrend Under Pressure" to a confirmed **"MARKET IN DOWNTREND."** This shift is driven by a severe negative catalyst breaking out overnight: **WSJ reports that Nvidia’s board is preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang** (CNBC/MarketWatch, 0.3h–1.5h ago). Consequently, **Nvidia (NVDA) has sunk 8% after hours**, dragging down Nasdaq futures. This is not merely a valuation correction; it is a leadership vacuum at the center of the AI trade, fundamentally altering the risk profile of the entire growth complex. Yesterday’s hot CPI data (0.4% MoM) is now secondary to this structural break in the market’s primary engine.

**2. MARKET REGIME ASSESSMENT**
The tape is signaling capitulation rather than consolidation. QQQ is down 11.18% from its highs and has decisively broken below both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages, confirming a deep correction phase. While the VIX’s 10% drop yesterday suggested calm, that calm has been shattered by the Nvidia leadership shakeup, indicating that the market is no longer de-risking gradually but is under active selling pressure. The 10Y yield at 5.0% provides no tailwind; in fact, it acts as a ceiling on multiple expansion, meaning any rebound will be fragile unless panic selling halts.

**3. SECTOR LEADERSHIP**
**Rotation is defensive, not cyclical.** Healthcare (XLV, +0.29% 1D) and Financials (XLF, +0.52% 1D) are the only sectors showing positive momentum today, while Technology (XLK) is the clear laggard at -1.15%. The divergence is stark: Semiconductors (SOXX) are down 19.15% over the last month, trading at a steep discount to the broader market, reflecting the severity of the AI trade unwind. Defensive staples and healthcare are absorbing the inflows from fleeing tech money. Note that Communication Services (XLC) was a relative leader last week (+2.67% 1W) but has now flipped into softness (0.1D 0.37%), showing that even resilient growth proxies are under pressure.

**4. NEWS-DRIVEN RISKS & CATALYSTS**
* **Nvidia CEO Ouster (Immediate Risk):** The headline "Nvidia prepares to oust Jensen Huang" (WSJ via CNBC) creates extreme near-term volatility. The "too big to fail" AI financing web (CNBC Analysis) suggests that if NVDA slides below key support, contagion to other megacap tech (META, MSFT) is highly probable. *Action:* Do not bottom-fish NVDA today.
* **Meta Infrastructure Leadership Change (Secondary Risk):** Meta’s head of infrastructure is leaving, to be replaced by an ex-AWS exec (CNBC, 1.1h ago). While not as catastrophic as the Nvidia news, it signals strategic instability in the hyperscaler capex cycle, reinforcing the narrative that the "infinite budget" era of AI spending is facing internal scrutiny.
* **December Macro Gauntlet (Upcoming Catalysts):** With **NFP on Dec 4** and **FOMC on Dec 9** upcoming, the market enters a high-stress window in a downtrend. The last CPI print was hot (0.4% MoM), and yields are at 5.0%. A soft jobs report could trigger a "growth scare" (recession fears), while a hot report confirms stagflation. There is no "good" scenario for growth stocks in December.

**5. GROWTH STOCK ENVIRONMENT**
**Poor.** The environment is hostile to Minervini-style trend following. QQQ is trending down, breaking key long-term support. NVDA, the sector’s anchor, is undergoing a leadership crisis. When the leader breaks, the followers (SOXX peers, smaller AI names) typically overshoot downward. The VIX is falling *into* this news, which is a classic warning sign of complacency being about to be punished. There is no "bargain" here; this is a regime change from expansion to contraction/de-risking.

**6. POSTURE RECOMMENDATION**
**DEFENSIVE**
* **Equity Allocation:** < 40%
* **Change from Yesterday:** **Aggressive Shift.** Yesterday’s posture was likely Neutral/Moderaetly Exposed due to lower VIX. Today, that is dangerously outdated.
